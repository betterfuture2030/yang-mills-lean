## 1. Purpose of this library

This library is the evidence base for a project to state, and eventually prove, the Clay Millennium problem "Yang–Mills existence and mass gap" in Lean 4. It serves three uses:

1. **Fix the target.** The first deliverable is a faithful Lean problem statement ("option 1"). It must match Jaffe–Witten clause by clause: every compact simple $G$, $\mathbb R^4$, Wightman/OS axioms, gauge-invariant local fields, asymptotic-freedom short-distance behaviour, and a spectral mass gap. It must be designed so that OpenAI's expected Lean release, an existence-only claim for pure YM, can be compared against it mechanically. Notes 01, 02, 04 and 10 serve this.
2. **Map the mathematics.** Each candidate proof route is mapped to the published research frontier, with what is proven, what is claimed and what is open:
   - Balaban-style lattice RG for existence;
   - constructive-QFT tools;
   - rigorous lattice results;
   - stochastic quantization;
   - mass-gap mechanisms;
   - the 2023–2026 frontier and its claimed solutions.

   Notes 05–09 and 11 serve this.
3. **Guide how AI is used.** OpenAI's Navier–Stokes agent swarm and its public proof repositories are documented as models of process, not as mathematical inputs: target freezing, formulation portfolios, consolidation, Lean/Comparator acceptance gates, and the failure modes (scope loopholes, Lean/paper drift, unreadable certificates). Notes 03 and 04 serve this.

**Conventions.**
- Every note tags claims by status. The tags are THM/T, CLAIM/C, HEUR/H, PREPRINT/T-pre, OPEN, AI-ASSESSED labels and others; each note's header defines its legend. Every note ends with a verification log, which records the reference checks made, the corrections applied and the residual concerns.
- In the bibliography below, **verified** means a note confirmed the item exists and its metadata is right (arXiv API, Crossref, publisher or GitHub page, or a live fetch). It does *not* mean the mathematics was checked. **UNVERIFIED** means no note could confirm the item. **mixed** means the notes disagree.
- AI-generated manuscripts and announced-but-unreleased proofs (OpenAI's 1,147 pages; Sussman–Alpöge; Aronow–Lopatto) are claims, not results.

**Known access gaps.**
- openai.com returned HTTP 403, so the 26 Oct 2026 announcement was rebuilt from secondary sources.
- No Balaban CMP paper was read in full: Project Euclid sat behind a bot wall. Note 05 relies on expositions and on secondhand transcriptions.
- Paywalled reviews were not read, notably Douglas, *Nat. Rev. Phys.* 2026.

## 4. The 25 most important references

1. **[Jaffe2000](#jaffe2000)** — A. Jaffe, E. Witten (2000), *Quantum Yang–Mills Theory (official Clay Millennium Prize problem description)*. The official problem statement. Every clause of the Lean target (G, R⁴, axioms, field content, AF, spectral gap, the no-weak-existence footnote) is taken from it.
2. **[Douglas2004](#douglas2004)** — M. R. Douglas (2004), *Report on the Status of the Yang–Mills Millenium Prize Problem*. Clay status report by a current committee member. It identifies existence with the OS axioms, says the lattice is optional, and names Balaban and MRS as the state of the art.
3. **[Osterwalder1975](#osterwalder1975)** — K. Osterwalder, R. Schrader (1975), *Axioms for Euclidean Green's functions II*. OS II, the reconstruction theorem the problem actually needs: linear growth E0′ repairs OS I's Lemma 8.8. Read it with [Osterwalder1973](#osterwalder1973).
4. **[Kravchuk2021](#kravchuk2021) ([PDF](pdfs/2104.02090.pdf))** — P. Kravchuk, J. Qiao, S. Rychkov (2021), *Distributions in CFT II. Minkowski space*.
